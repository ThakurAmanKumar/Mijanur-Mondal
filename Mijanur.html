Current Position & Research Interest

Current Position: Doctoral Researcher (PhD Scholar), Department of Environmental Science & Engineering, SRM University-AP, Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh, India[cite: 1].

Research Interest:

  • Hydroclimatic variability and its influence on surface water-groundwater interactions and coastal aquifer dynamics[cite: 1].
  • Application of geospatial technologies (GIS and remote sensing) and machine learning for groundwater vulnerability assessment and spatial modelling[cite: 1].
  • Application of stable water isotopes ($\delta^{2}H$ and $\delta^{18}O$) and radon/radium isotopes ($^{222}Rn$ and $^{226}Ra$) to investigate groundwater-surface water interactions and submarine groundwater discharge[cite: 1].
  • Hydrogeochemical processes, solute transport, and groundwater salinization in coastal aquifer systems[cite: 1].
